# My people

> Who matters, how to treat them, and what is currently open with each.
> The twin reads this before drafting anything addressed to a person.

## Format

### [Name] — [relationship, e.g. co-founder / investor / client]
- **Tone with them:** [formal | casual | very casual]
- **Context:** [what we're working on together]
- **Open threads:** [what's outstanding in either direction]
- **Never:** [anything the twin must not do or say here]

---

### [Example] — client
- **Tone with them:** warm but precise, they read carefully
- **Context:** Q3 delivery
- **Open threads:** owes me the brief; I owe them a revised timeline
- **Never:** commit to dates without checking with me first
